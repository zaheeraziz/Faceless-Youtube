# Social Platform Monetization for Faceless Educational Video

Updated: August 2026. Availability varies by country and account; platforms can change or invite creators selectively.

| Platform | Direct platform income | Public entry threshold | Fit for our business |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube | Long-form/Shorts ads, Premium, memberships, Supers, Shopping | Ads: 1K subscribers + 4K public watch hours/12 months, or 10M Shorts views/90 days | **Primary** |
| Facebook | Content Monetization across Reels, long videos, photos and text; Stars; brands | Content Monetization access/eligibility is not consistently guaranteed publicly | **Secondary distribution** |
| TikTok | Creator Rewards, LIVE gifts/subscriptions, brands | Rewards: 10K followers + 100K views/30 days; eligible region; original 1+ minute videos | **Discovery/testing** |
| Instagram | Subscriptions, Gifts, brand partnerships/Creator Marketplace; availability varies | Feature-, country-, and account-dependent; no dependable general ad-share threshold | **Brand/discovery** |
| Snapchat | Story/Spotlight ad share and brands | Invitation; 50K followers + 15K watch hours/28 days, including 3K Spotlight; Snap Star | **Low priority** |
| X | Creator revenue sharing, subscriptions | Premium subscription + 5M organic impressions/3 months; other requirements apply | **Low priority** |
| LinkedIn | Brand marketplace, leads, services | Marketplace invite; indicated criteria include 5K followers, 12 posts and 45K impressions/90 days | **Poor niche fit** |

## Key economics

- YouTube publishes a 55% creator share of net Watch Page ad revenue and 45% of allocated Shorts revenue.
- TikTok rewards only qualified views on eligible original videos of at least one minute; sponsored videos do not qualify for Creator Rewards.
- Facebook's unified program can monetize several formats, but access is less predictable than YouTube YPP.
- Instagram is stronger for sponsorships, audience growth and downstream commerce than dependable view-based payouts.
- Snapchat's threshold is high for a new educational brand.

## Recommended platform strategy

1. Build **10–18 minute YouTube episodes** as the core product and monetization asset.
2. Derive original, reformatted 60–90 second cuts for TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts.
3. Use short platforms to test hooks and acquire viewers—not as the financial foundation.
4. Upload clean masters without watermarks; adapt pacing, captions and framing per platform.
5. Keep scripts, narration and visual rights documentation. Every platform increasingly penalizes copied, minimally modified or mass-produced content.

## Bottom line

For faceless educational documentaries, YouTube offers the clearest and most durable direct monetization system. Facebook is the best second direct-revenue possibility, but access is less predictable. TikTok and Instagram are primarily discovery and sponsorship channels for this project.
