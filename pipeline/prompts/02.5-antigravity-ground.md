# Step 2.5 — Antigravity grounds the accuracy claims

- **Role:** Antigravity CLI (`agy -p`) — fallback: Gemini via Vertex AI
- **Input:** candidate accuracy claims from Step 2
- **Output:** a grounded verdict + source per claim, folded into `feedback.json`'s `grounded` field

## Instructions

For each candidate claim, search and return:

- `verdict`: `supported`, `contradicted`, or `unverifiable`.
- `source`: the specific source. Prefer primary/authoritative sources — museums, universities, archives, government cultural institutions, per `PROJECT-CONTEXT.md`'s sourcing bar.
- A one-line explanation when the verdict is `contradicted` or `unverifiable`.

Do not soften a `contradicted` verdict because the claim seems minor. A small wrong fact still gets flagged — the point of this step is that "both models agree" is not the bar a fact has to clear.

This step runs once per accuracy candidate, not once per draft — keep each call small and specific.
