# Audience Packaging Test

## Purpose

Validate demand and packaging before buying tools or producing a full video.

## Who to test

20 adults who already watch science, history, or educational YouTube. Do not rely only on friends and family.

## Packages

Randomize the order for each respondent.

| Package | Title | Thumbnail |
|---|---|---|
| A | Before GPS, the Sky Was the Map | `validation/thumbnails/01-fixed-star.svg` |
| B | How Sailors Found Their Way Using Only the Stars | `validation/thumbnails/02-lost-found.svg` |
| C | How One Star Could Tell You Where You Were | `validation/thumbnails/03-no-gps.svg` |

Show each thumbnail at phone-feed size, with its title.

## Ask exactly

1. Which one would you click first?
2. What do you expect the video to show or explain?
3. What, if anything, is confusing or misleading?
4. Would you watch this for 8–12 minutes? Why?

Do not explain the channel idea before they answer.

## Pass gate

Proceed to a low-cost pilot only if:

- One package wins at least 8 of 20 first-click votes (40%).
- At least 70% of that package's voters correctly expect a story about historical star navigation.
- Feedback reveals no major misleading promise.

If no package passes, revise the promise and packaging—not the production tools. After a winner emerges, test its title and thumbnail separately to learn which element caused the result.
